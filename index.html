HOW TO PLAY VELOCITY ARENA CONTROLS · CLASHES · MULTIPLAYER

A free online movement FPS

Play private 1v1 duels on three arenas. Create a room, share its code, and both ready up. The first player to win five rounds wins the match. Sign in to save earned coins, unlocked weapons and your loadout, or play as a guest.

Speed wins a melee clash

Sprint with Shift, slide with C or Ctrl, then jump to carry your momentum. Air-strafe to change your route. When two nearby, facing players swing melee weapons within 0.12 seconds, their attacks clash. A lead of at least 1 m/s wins and stuns the slower player for 0.75 seconds. Close ties repel both players.

Parry, practice, improve

With the katana, press F or right mouse to guard against bullets, arrows and flames. Rockets and melee bypass the guard. Use Free Play to practice without bots, or face ECHO in solo. WASD moves, Space jumps and double-jumps, left mouse attacks, R reloads, and 1–5 selects weapons. Desktop keyboard and mouse recommended.
